# AuraState Study Guide

## How to Use AuraState

### 1. Complete Onboarding
- Select your region and province (locks curriculum)
- Choose your grade (10, 11, or 12)
- Pick your stream (science, commerce, humanities)
- Select 7 subjects (mandatory + electives)
- Set your weak points (starts at 20% mastery)

### 2. Study with Chronos
- Your calendar auto-generates based on mastery
- Weak subjects get more time
- Review sprints injected for concepts below 34.85%
- Exam countdown shows urgency

### 3. Use the Socratic Vault
- Pick a topic and start an interview
- AI asks questions based on your answers
- Two modes: Full Detail (deep learning) or Exam Summary (rapid review)
- Voice input available

### 4. Focus Sessions
- Start a session and stay focused
- Tab switching triggers meltdown (10s grace)
- XP earned based on time and consistency
- Streak bonuses: 3 days = 1.1x, 7 days = 1.25x, 30 days = 2x

### 5. Track Mastery
- Quiz yourself on any topic
- Mastery updates with weighted rolling average
- Weak points auto-detected below 34.85%
- Calendar adjusts schedule based on results

### 6. Join War Rooms
- Study with classmates in subject-specific rooms
- Ask questions, share tips
- Real-time messaging

## Study Tips

- **Consistency > Intensity:** 1 hour daily beats 7 hours on Sunday
- **Use Exam Mode:** Before tests, switch to exam summary for rapid review
- **Trust the Streak:** The multiplier system rewards daily effort
- **Review Weak Points:** Focus on concepts below 34.85%
- **Use Flashcards:** AI generates exam-focused cards for any topic
